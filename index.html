CODeL Courseware

A working prototype of the proposed digital courseware service, with every CODeL programme and course from the September 2026 course list. Prepared by Dr Beatrice Torto and Dr Enusah Abdulai.

Sign in Browse all programmes
Concept prototype, not a live service. Programme and course titles come from CODeL’s September 2026 list. Course-book text, questions, people and index numbers are fictional samples. Everything you do is saved only in this browser, so several accounts can be tried on one device. Use Screens and Next at the bottom of every page to move around.

Explore the features

Sign in

1000 demo students and 74 staff accounts across 16 study centres, each with an index number or staff ID and password.

Student dashboard

Current and read-only courses, reading progress, exams, group meetings and notifications for the signed-in student.

Reader: scroll view

Bookmarks, highlights, notes, search, read-aloud, text size and night mode.

Reader: flip-book view

Pages turn like a printed book. Toggle between the two views; page numbers match.

Quizzes

Self-checks for every course, with review, explanations and best scores. Tutors build their own.

Online exams

Timed, one attempt, auto-submit, integrity log and results release.

Past questions

A bank of past questions with marking guides and practice answers.

AI study assistant

Reads the whole course book and past questions; cites unit, section and page.

Course discussion

Unit threads, tutor answers, pinning, reporting and moderation.

Study groups

Student-built workspaces: invite by index number, chat, shared notes, resources and meetings.

Staff views

Tutors, coordinators, study-centre coordinators, helpdesk and administrators.

Print a study copy

Watermarked with name and index number; two copies per book; print log.
