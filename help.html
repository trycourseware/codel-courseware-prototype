Help and support

?How do I read my course books offline?
Open the course and choose Download. In the Moodle app, books and audio are small; download videos on Wi-Fi where possible. Your notes sync when you reconnect.
?What is the difference between the scroll view and the flip-book view?
Both show the same book with the same page numbers. The scroll view is best on phones; the flip-book view turns pages like a printed book. Switch with the Scroll and Flip buttons at the top of the reader; your choice is remembered.
?Can I print my course book?
Yes. During the semester of registration you may print a personal study copy at your own cost, up to two copies per book. Every page carries your name and index number and a copy ID. Printing for sale is prohibited.
?What happens to my books after the semester?
You keep read-only online access for one further semester, with your bookmarks and notes. Downloading, printing and the AI assistant are not available in that period.
?How do quizzes and online exams work?
Practice quizzes can be taken as often as you like and your best score is kept. Online exams have an opening window, a time limit and one attempt; they are submitted automatically when time runs out. The AI assistant and forums are switched off during an exam.
?What are study groups?
Private workspaces you create with classmates: invite them by index number, chat, keep shared notes, share resources and plan meetings. They are separate from the course discussion and tutors do not see them.
?What is the AI study assistant?
An optional paid service that answers questions from your approved course books and the past-questions bank, and shows the unit, section and page it used. It is not for assessed work and is switched off during exams.
?I cannot see a course I registered for.
Courses appear automatically from your registration, usually within an hour. If a course is still missing, open a helpdesk ticket and quote your index number.

Contact the helpdesk

Every request gets a reference number so you can follow it until it is resolved.

Report a serious concern anonymously

Reports go to an independent unit through a separate, secure system (GlobaLeaks). You receive a 16-digit receipt to follow up without revealing who you are. Nothing about your report is recorded in CODeL Courseware.
